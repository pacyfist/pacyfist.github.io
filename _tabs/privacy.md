## Cookies

This site sets two kinds of cookies.

**Needed to run the site.** These remember small things, like whether you picked
light or dark mode. Nothing is sent anywhere. They are always on.

**Visit stats.** Google Analytics counts visits and shows me which posts people
read. It is off until you turn it on. If you say no, the Google script is never
loaded at all.

## What I do with the data

I look at visit numbers to see which posts are worth writing more of. That is
all. I do not sell anything, I do not run ads, and I do not build profiles.

If you turn on visit stats, Google gets your IP address and some details about
your browser. You can read what Google does with it in their
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your choices

You can change your mind at any time with the button above, or by clearing this
site's cookies in your browser.

If you want to know what I hold about you, or want it removed, email me at the
address in the sidebar.
